Unit Economics of Agentic AI

Organizations pay for AI work without being able to tell whether it was worth the cost: current measures record spend rather than yield, and better spend tooling alone will not resolve that limitation.

“TokenOps maximizes the fraction of spend that converts into accepted, valuable, autonomously-completed outcomes; that composite conversion rate is token yield, the metric tracked across this hub.”
— DEFINITION USED THROUGHOUT THE SITE

Formally, token yield = Agent Efficiency Score (AES) = ACR × TER × VGR, which differs from raw acceptance. The Agent Loop explains why accepted output alone is insufficient.
